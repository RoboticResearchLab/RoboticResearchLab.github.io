Support RRL

Sponsorship opportunities

Organizations can work with the lab in several ways. Reach out through the contact page once an official email is published, or via the GitHub organization.

Hardware sponsorship

Robot platforms, sensors, compute modules, grippers, and industrial equipment that expand what the lab can prototype and evaluate.

Software and cloud resources

Licenses, simulation seats, dataset hosting, and cloud GPU/CPU allocations for training, CI, and large-scale experiments.

Research collaboration

Joint projects, internships, shared testbeds, and co-authored studies on autonomy, perception, and industrial systems.

Financial support

Unrestricted or project-scoped gifts that fund students, travel, competition logistics, and laboratory operations.

Industrial partnerships

Problem statements from the factory floor, access to cells and data (under agreement), and pathways from research software to deployed automation.
