Agenda

Research areas

RRL organizes its work around eight technical areas. Descriptions below are disciplinary, not claims about a specific deployed system.

Autonomous Robotics

Methods for robots that sense, localize, plan, and act with limited teleoperation. This includes mapping, obstacle avoidance, task-level autonomy, and recovery when models of the world are incomplete.

SLAM Planning Control

Related projects: Autonomous Mobile Robots

ROS 2

Robot Operating System 2 is the integration layer we use for distributed nodes, DDS-based communication, lifecycle management, and reusable packages across perception, navigation, and manipulation stacks.

rclcpp / rclpy DDS Nav2

Related projects: Platform categories

Computer Vision

Geometric and learning-based vision for object detection, 6-DoF pose, tracking, and scene understanding. We care about latency, calibration, and robustness under industrial lighting and clutter.

OpenCV Pose estimation RGB-D

Related projects: Computer Vision

Industrial Automation

Connecting robot cells to production logic: sequencing, interlocks, human–machine interfaces, and the practical constraints of commissioning, maintainability, and functional safety.

PLC Workcells HMI

Related projects: Industrial Robotics, RoboCup @Work

Edge Computing

Onboard and near-machine compute for inference, sensor processing, and control loops that cannot tolerate round-trips to the cloud. Topics include GPU/NPU deployment, determinism, and resource isolation.

Jetson-class Inference RT

Related projects: Autonomous Mobile Robots

Industrial Communication

Fieldbuses and industrial Ethernet used to integrate robots, drives, and I/O. Research interest includes time-sensitive networking, protocol gateways, and reliable ROS 2 bridges to shop-floor systems.

EtherCAT PROFINET OPC UA

Related projects: Industrial Robotics

Robot Simulation

Physics-based and kinematic simulation for software-in-the-loop testing, synthetic data, and operator training. We treat sim-to-real gap as an experimental variable, not a slogan.

Gazebo URDF / SDF HIL

Related projects: Simulation

AI for Robotics

Learning methods applied to perception, skill policies, and decision-making, with emphasis on data efficiency, safety constraints, and systems that remain inspectable when they fail.

Learning Policies Perception

Related projects: Computer Vision, Manipulation
