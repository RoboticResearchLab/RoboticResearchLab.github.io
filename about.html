About RRL

A laboratory for intelligent machines

Robotic Research Lab exists to design, build, and study robotic systems that can operate usefully in the physical world — in laboratories, factories, and unstructured environments.

Who We Are

Researchers and engineers building robots

RRL is a robotics research team working on autonomous systems, industrial automation, computer vision, ROS 2, edge computing, and industrial communication. We treat hardware, software, and evaluation as one stack: platforms, perception, control, and the networks that connect them.

Our Mission

Make autonomy reliable enough to deploy

Our mission is to advance methods and systems that let robots perceive their surroundings, plan under uncertainty, and execute tasks with a level of robustness suitable for research prototypes and industrial settings.

We care about reproducibility, open tooling where appropriate, and clear interfaces between algorithms and machines.

Our Vision

Intelligent systems that work beside people and plants

We envision robots that can be commissioned, inspected, and improved like other engineered systems — not black boxes. That means strong perception, disciplined software (including ROS 2), and compute that can run at the edge, close to sensors and actuators.

Research Philosophy

Theory, platforms, and measurement

RRL’s approach is to pair algorithmic work with physical and simulated robots. Simulation accelerates iteration; hardware exposes timing, sensing, and communication constraints that papers can otherwise hide. Industrial communication and automation problems are treated as first-class research, not afterthoughts.
